How it works

How the referral system runs itself

You set a business up once. After that, six AI agents do the routine work, and a person only steps in to approve what matters.

The four steps

From “tell a friend” to a working program

1
Step 1

Point it at a business

Type a name, paste a website or choose one of 37 trade packs. The seller says it works out what's sold and what a customer is worth in about 15 seconds.

2
Step 2

Let the program draft itself

It drafts the friend's offer, the advocate's reward, the messages, the offer page and the QR kit. Everything is editable, and nothing goes live until you press Launch.

3
Step 3

The agents go to work

The Recruiter signs up advocates, the Nudge agent prompts them to share at the right moment, and the Verifier confirms who actually bought.

4
Step 4

Rewards pay, results report

The Fraud agent scores each claim, the Payout agent releases rewards, and the Report agent sends the month's results.

Behind the scenes

Six agents, six jobs

These are the roles the seller describes. AI drafts and explains; fixed rules, real confirmations and a person's approval decide who gets paid.

Recruiter agent

Reviews the customer list, Google reviews and social mentions, then invites likely advocates with their own link, code and QR.

Nudge agent

Times each ask for when an advocate is most likely to act, follows up with quiet ones, and never texts between 9pm and 9am.

Verifier agent

Confirms who really bought: a staff tap at the till, a unique code, a paid store order or the thank-you page.

Fraud agent

Scores every claim from 0 to 100 with a plain-English reason. Clean claims clear; risky ones wait for one tap.

Payout agent

Releases the reward, from store credit to cash or a gift card, after the refund window. Cash always waits for a person.

Report agent

Writes the monthly report from real numbers: new customers, referred revenue and cost per customer.

Daily routine

What the owner actually does

Once a campaign is live, the hands-on work is small and predictable:

  • Glance at the digest of anything waiting for approval
  • Approve or block flagged claims with one tap
  • Approve cash rewards, which always need a person
  • Read the monthly report and tweak the offer if needed

Running more than one business or client? See the focus areas for how each setup works.

Built-in safeguards

  • Opt-in only, with STOP honoured immediately
  • Quiet hours from 9pm to 9am in each business's time zone
  • Every claim fraud-scored with a reason
  • Rewards held through the refund window; no payouts on refunded sales
  • A budget ceiling that pauses the campaign when it's reached

Good to know

Texts are sent from each business's own Twilio number, so Twilio's normal charges apply. Cash and gift-card payouts with Stripe are part of the Pro plan, which the Bundle includes.
