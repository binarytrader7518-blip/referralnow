Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

This is a static information website. We don't run user accounts, contact forms, comments or analytics, and we don't collect personal information directly.

Information stored in your browser

To avoid showing the promotional pop-up repeatedly, the site stores a timestamp in your browser's local storage, and a session flag if you close the notification card. Nothing is sent to us.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts delivers the site's typefaces, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Product images load from the seller's website (referralnow.co).
  • Hosting is provided by Vercel, which may keep standard server logs.
  • Affiliate links lead to JVZoo and the seller's site, which use their own cookies and tracking under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can clear local storage in your browser settings at any time, and block third-party cookies if you prefer.
