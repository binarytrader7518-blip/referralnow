What it is

A complete referral program, built and run for you

ReferralNow is a web app that finds the customers already recommending a business, gives each of them a personal link and code, confirms which referred friends actually bought, and pays the reward. Then it invites every new customer to become the next advocate.

How it works

From a business name to a live program in four steps

Steps one and two take minutes. After that, the agents handle the daily work and the owner approves what matters.

1

Point it at a business

Type a name, paste a website or choose one of 37 trade packs. The seller says it works out what's sold and what a customer is worth in about 15 seconds.

2

Let the program draft itself

It drafts the friend's offer, the advocate's reward, the messages, the offer page and the QR kit. Everything is editable, and nothing goes live until you press Launch.

3

The agents go to work

The Recruiter signs up advocates, the Nudge agent prompts them to share at the right moment, and the Verifier confirms who actually bought.

4

Rewards pay, results report

The Fraud agent scores each claim, the Payout agent releases rewards, and the Report agent sends the month's results.

Good habits

Software amplifies referrals. It can't create them.

People recommend businesses that treat them well. ReferralNow makes it easy to ask, track and reward, but the best results come from pairing it with a few simple habits:

  • Ask right after a good experience, not weeks later
  • Offer a reward that fits your margins and your customers
  • Thank advocates quickly once a sale is confirmed
  • Review the monthly report and adjust the offer, not just the budget
