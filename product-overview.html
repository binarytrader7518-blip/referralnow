Introduction

The short version

ReferralNow is web-based software for running a referral program. You point it at a business, it drafts the offer and messages, and a set of AI agents handle the routine work: finding likely advocates, reminding them to share, confirming real purchases, scoring claims for fraud, releasing rewards and reporting results.

The Referral Now Bundle is the package that includes all of it. The front end (Premium) and the four upgrades (Pro, Unlimited, Agency and Reseller) normally sell separately. The Bundle combines them on one login, so you can run referral programs in your own businesses, for clients, and as accounts you sell.

Want to know what that means day to day? Read the benefits or how it works.
